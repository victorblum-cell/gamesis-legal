Frequently Asked

How do I play?
Each level shows a target shape. Place your seeds on the grid and press start – they grow according to a hidden rule. Your goal is to make them match the target shape exactly. Figuring out the rule is the challenge!

My progress or statistics disappeared.
Your progress and statistics are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data will remove them. Please contact us if something looks wrong.

How can I change my ad consent choice?
You can update your advertising consent at any time in the app's settings.
